Shelbourne FC

A recruitment report built around one question: how can Shelbourne replace the attacking production lost with Harry Wood while improving the squad's tactical balance and long-term value?

Overview

Replacing more than a position.

Across the first 26 league matches in the supplied dataset, Shelbourne scored 36 goals and conceded 38. They averaged 53.1% possession and 14 shots per match. The clearest recruitment issue is the departure of Harry Wood to Stockport County.

Before leaving, Wood had scored 10 league goals and supplied five assists. He also led Shelbourne for key passes, big chances created, shots and successful dribbles. The preferred solution does not need to be an exact statistical clone; the aim is to restore the overall attacking output while improving age profile and long-term value.

Playing model

What the role needs to do

In possession

  • Stay comfortable receiving and retaining the ball in a side averaging more than 50% possession.
  • Receive between the lines and carry through pressure.
  • Create chances independently.
  • Operate centrally or start wide before moving inside.
  • Combine goal threat with creation or ball progression.

Out of possession

  • Press aggressively after possession is lost.
  • Retain positional discipline.
  • Accelerate into space after turnovers.
  • Set-piece delivery is useful, but secondary to open-play creation and progression.

Target A

Ben McCormack

The report rates McCormack at 8.8/10 for role fit. At Bray Wanderers he had 10 goals and four assists, averaged 1.8 key passes and five recoveries per 90, and offered strong box-arrival threat, a good age/resale profile and low adaptation risk.

What he adds

  • 10 goals + 4 assists
  • 1.8 key passes /90
  • 5 recoveries /90
  • Strong box-arrival threat
  • Good age + resale profile
  • Low adaptation risk

Concerns

  • Step up from First Division
  • Less 1v1 output than Wood
  • Not a like-for-like creator
  • Scoring may dip at higher level
Value assessment: 8.9/10 overall. Ideal deal: free / pre-contract. Good deal: €0–€20k. Maximum: €25k. Prioritise wages and signing terms over a significant transfer fee.
