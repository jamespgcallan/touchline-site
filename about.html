About the publication

Football, beyond the scoreline.

Touchline Studios is an independent football publication looking at how clubs are built, how teams play and why the decisions around the game matter.

What Touchline covers

Touchline publishes original analysis on the League of Ireland and wider football, with a particular focus on club strategy, ownership, recruitment, academies, commercial thinking and tactical game models.

The aim is simple: make the structures behind football understandable without stripping the fun out of it. Some pieces are timely reactions to what is happening now. Others are longer features designed to explain the systems, decisions and ideas sitting underneath the headlines.

Our main areas

Irish Football

League of Ireland clubs, academies, ownership, recruitment, finance and development.

Touchline Tactics

Long-form analysis of game models, pressing, build-up structures and coaching ideas.

Club Strategy

How clubs organise departments, generate revenue, recruit, develop players and plan for the long term.

Who is behind it

Touchline Studios was founded and is written by James Callan. Every article carries a clear byline and publication date, and James' author page sets out his background and areas of work.
