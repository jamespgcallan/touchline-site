Overview

The gap is an established immediate No.1.

Chelsea's goalkeeper question is more specific than simply replacing Robert Sánchez. Sánchez remains a capable Premier League goalkeeper and his underlying 2025/26 numbers were better than some of the criticism suggests. The issue is whether the position is good enough for where Chelsea want to go.

Mike Penders changes the recruitment completely. At 21, 2.00m and already coming off a full Ligue 1 season, Chelsea already own a genuine long-term goalkeeper. The priority should therefore be an immediate upgrade without closing Penders' pathway.

Recruitment objective

What the new goalkeeper should improve

  • Improve shot stopping
  • Reduce costly errors
  • Remain strong under pressure
  • Distribute accurately
  • Defend space behind the back three
  • Command crosses
  • Be ready to start immediately
  • Offer proven performance and market value rather than age alone
Playing model

In possession and out of possession

In possession

Sánchez completed 66.7% of his passes and 32.1% of his long balls in 2025/26. The new goalkeeper must be comfortable receiving under pressure, avoid central turnovers, switch play quickly, find midfielders early and go longer when the short option is closed.

Distribution should create an advantage. The goalkeeper should be capable of turning a regain into an attack rather than simply restarting possession.

Out of possession

Sánchez's benchmark was a 67.6% save percentage and +1.70 goals prevented. The replacement should show a clear statistical argument while also starting high, attacking through balls, leaving the box, covering centre-backs and making decisions early.

Height, high claims and decision-making under contact remain important because Chelsea cannot improve shot stopping while weakening significantly in the air.

Markets assessed

Broad search, with Serie A a priority

The report assessed the Premier League, Championship, Serie A, Serie B, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, La Liga, Portugal, the Netherlands and Chelsea's internal options. The search prioritised goalkeepers under 28, preferably 23–26, with significant senior minutes and a transfer cost in the £10m–£25m range.

Target A

Filip Stanković

Stanković is the strongest statistical value option in the search. His 2025/26 season combined a 77.2% save rate, +9.02 goals prevented, 78.4% pass accuracy and 43.1% long-ball accuracy. That gives Chelsea a potential upgrade in both shot stopping and build-up.

Verdict: Primary value target. The main concern is the jump from Serie B, while Venezia's new deal until 2030 strengthens their negotiating position.
Target B

Marco Carnesecchi

Carnesecchi is the safer proven option. His 2025/26 Serie A season included a 76% save rate, +7.07 goals prevented, 13 clean sheets and zero errors leading to goals. His distribution is less impressive than Stanković's, but he offers stronger top-flight evidence and less projection risk.

Verdict: Best proven alternative. Lower-risk option with elite Serie A shot-stopping numbers.
