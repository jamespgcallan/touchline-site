Overview

The gap is an established immediate No.1.

Chelsea's goalkeeper question is more specific than simply replacing Robert Sánchez. Sánchez remains a capable Premier League goalkeeper and his underlying 2025/26 numbers were better than some of the criticism suggests. The issue is whether the position is good enough for where Chelsea want to go.

Mike Penders changes the recruitment completely. At 21, 2.00m and already coming off a full Ligue 1 season, Chelsea already own a genuine long-term goalkeeper. That means the priority should not be another expensive development signing. It should be finding someone capable of improving the team immediately without closing Penders' pathway.

Recruitment objective

What the new goalkeeper should improve

  • Improve shot stopping
  • Reduce costly errors
  • Remain strong under pressure
  • Distribute accurately
  • Defend space behind the back three
  • Command crosses
  • Be ready to start immediately
  • Focus on proven performance and market value rather than age alone
Playing model

In possession

Build-up

Sánchez completed 66.7% of his passes and 32.1% of his long balls in the 2025/26 league season. Those figures leave room to improve. The new goalkeeper must be comfortable receiving under pressure without becoming overly safe in possession.

Progression

Alonso's Chelsea are looking to move the ball forward more quickly. That increases the importance of accurate medium and long distribution. Stanković completed 78.4% of his passes and 43.1% of his long balls last season. The strongest target should give Chelsea another way through or over pressure.

Press resistance

  • Receive calmly
  • Recognise pressure
  • Avoid central turnovers
  • Switch play quickly
  • Find midfielders early
  • Go longer when the short option is closed

Transition to attack

Distribution should create an advantage. The goalkeeper should be capable of turning a regain into an attack rather than simply restarting possession.

Playing model

Out of possession

Shot stopping

Sánchez's 2025/26 benchmark was a 67.6% save percentage and +1.70 goals prevented. Chelsea should not recruit based on reputation alone. Carnesecchi recorded a save rate around 77% while preventing more than seven goals above expectation. That is the type of improvement the search should identify.

Defensive block

  • Start high
  • Attack through balls
  • Leave the box
  • Cover centre-backs
  • Make decisions early

Transitions

  • Recognise danger quickly
  • Close attackers
  • Protect space outside the box
  • Dominate one-v-ones
  • Restart play immediately

Set pieces / box control

Sánchez remains useful from crosses. The replacement cannot improve shot stopping while weakening Chelsea significantly in the air. Height, high claims and decision-making under contact remain important.

What is missing?

Raise the overall level

The gap becomes clearer when Sánchez's profile is viewed as a whole. He still produced +1.70 goals prevented and 42 high claims, but only a 67.6% save percentage, 66.7% pass accuracy and 32.1% long-ball accuracy. Chelsea do not need a goalkeeper who is different in one area. They need one who raises the overall level.

Player fit

Target A — Filip Stanković, Venezia

Stanković is the strongest statistical value option in the search. His 2025/26 season combined a 77.2% save rate, +9.02 goals prevented, 78.4% pass accuracy and 43.1% long-ball accuracy. That gives Chelsea a potential upgrade in both shot stopping and build-up.

The main concern is the jump from Serie B, while Venezia's new deal until 2030 also strengthens their negotiating position.

Verdict: PRIMARY VALUE TARGET. Outstanding data profile with the age and upside to justify deeper scouting.
Player fit

Target B — Marco Carnesecchi, Atalanta

Carnesecchi is the safer proven option. His 2025/26 Serie A season included a 76% save rate, +7.07 goals prevented, 13 clean sheets and 0 errors leading to goals. The shot stopping and reliability are excellent.

His distribution is less impressive than Stanković's, but he offers stronger top-flight evidence and less projection risk.

Verdict: BEST PROVEN ALTERNATIVE. Lower-risk option with elite Serie A shot-stopping numbers.

The data points most strongly towards Filip Stanković. The strongest argument is not simply his 77.2% save rate, but the combination. He prevented +9.02 goals above expectation while also posting significantly better possession numbers than Sánchez. Chelsea would not simply be trading one goalkeeper strength for another. The profile potentially improves both shot stopping and build-up quality.

At 24, he also fits Chelsea's recruitment model far better than an older short-term solution. The obvious risk is Serie B translation. That should be investigated through video, shot-location analysis, one-v-one performance, cross claiming and pressure data before any decision. Statistically, he has done enough to earn first place.
